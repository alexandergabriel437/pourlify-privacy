Pourlify Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how the Pourlify mobile game ("the app", "we") handles information. We designed the app to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

The app saves your game progress (current level, lives, undo counts, sound settings and similar preferences) only on your own device. We do not receive or store this data on any server. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, including rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your location and your choices, ads may be personalized.

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you personally. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent form (provided through Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads load. You can review or change your choice at any time using "Privacy settings" in the app's Settings, when it is shown.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data handled by Google for advertising is subject to Google's own policies linked above.

6. Security

We do not operate servers that store your personal information. The app itself only reads and writes its data on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will show the latest version.
